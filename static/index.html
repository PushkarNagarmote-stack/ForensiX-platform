How ForensiX Fulfills Every Benchmark of Problem Statement PS-DFIR-04

Modern forensic laboratories and enterprise decommissioning pipelines encounter a critical dilemma: hardware must be permanently zeroed before disposal to avoid data leaks, yet critical digital evidence remains trapped within corrupted partitions. ForensiX systematically solves each objective of PS-DFIR-04:

ForensiX scans raw unallocated blocks to recover crucial files even when file allocation tables (FAT, NTFS, ext4) are destroyed, fulfilling the prerequisite to preserve evidence before sanitization.

2. Certified Multi-Standard Sanitization

Implements NIST SP 800-88 Clear & Purge, DoD 5220.22-M 3-pass, and SSD firmware purge routines, guaranteeing total eradication of sensitive remnant data.

3. Mathematical Shannon Entropy Proof

Calculates continuous Shannon entropy telemetry across all sectors post-wipe. Achieving H = 0.0000 mathematically confirms uniform zero distribution with zero hidden data leaks.

DUAL-LOOP LIFECYCLE

Bridging Evidence Recovery with Irreversible Sanitization

Traditional tools either destroy hardware without salvaging evidence, or salvage files while leaving sensitive data un-erased. ForensiX unites both into a single autonomous dual-loop pipeline.

🔍

Loop A: Pre-Decommission Carving

Evidence Salvage Phase

Before any media sanitization protocol executes, the ForensiX Carver performs a sector-by-sector deep byte search across unallocated clusters, slack space, and damaged partitions.

1 Magic Header Match: Identifies JPEG (`FF D8 FF`), PDF (`25 50 44 46`), PNG, and Office files without directory indexes.
2 Payload Reconstruction: Carves byte streams into validated files with calculated cryptographic SHA-256 hashes.
3 Custody Archival: Stores carved evidence in court-admissible evidence packages with chain of custody tracking.

Loop B: Certified Zero-Data Wipe

Irreversible Destruction Phase

Once evidence is secured, ForensiX executes certified multi-pass overwrites following NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1, DoD 5220.22-M, and SSD firmware sanitisers.

1 Multi-Pass Overwrite: Executes logical passes (zeros, pseudo-random, bitwise inversion, fixed byte).
2 Shannon Entropy Proof: Telemetry computes bit distributions across every block to prove entropy is strictly 0.0000.
3 Tamper-Proof Audit Certificate: Generates automated ReportLab PDF destruction certificates for regulatory sign-off.
